CONTROLS

Two players, one keyboard. Gamepads are picked up automatically if plugged in.

On a phone

  • Left stick: slide to move, push up to jump, down to crouch. Double-tap left or right to dash.
  • Right buttons: jab, punch, kick, high kick, and a held guard. Crouch while pressing punch for an uppercut, or kick for a sweep.
  • FINISH lights up once the meter is full. The ❚❚ button at the top pauses.
  • Landscape gives the fight more room, but portrait is framed for it too.

Rules that matter

  • Guard height: standing guard stops highs and mids, crouching guard stops lows and mids. Sweeps and low kicks go under a standing guard; high kicks sail over a crouching one.
  • Parry: a guard pressed within 6 frames of the hit reflects it and stuns the attacker. Holding guard does not re-arm it — you have to time the press.
  • Guard meter: blocking chips it away. Empty it and the guard breaks, leaving you stunned for most of a second.
  • Counters: hitting someone during their startup or recovery frames does 35% extra.
  • Combos: landing a hit lets you cancel the recovery into another move, up to four deep. Each extra hit scales the damage down.
  • Stamina: every attack costs it. Run out and you cannot attack or guard until you recover.
  • Finisher: fills from damage dealt and taken. It breaks guards outright and cannot be parried.
